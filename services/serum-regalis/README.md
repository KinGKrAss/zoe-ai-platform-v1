# Serum Regalis

Serum Regalis is represented in Z1 as a regulated health-products business domain.

The integration supports:

- product and batch records;
- supplier and partner records;
- regulatory-status fields;
- pharmacy / specialist-retail channels;
- jurisdiction review before activation;
- transaction evidence and audit references.

Cannabis and medicinal products remain subject to the applicable local rules. The system must not mark a partner as approved merely because it is listed here.

## Candidate partner

`Nobody's Place — Venlo, Netherlands` may be stored as a **candidate** partner record. No partnership, ownership, product authorization or payment acceptance is implied until separately verified and contractually agreed.
